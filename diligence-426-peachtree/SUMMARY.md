## Recommended offer & structure
- **Target $435,000–$440,000, $0 down**, with **(a) permit/records contingency**, **(b)
  septic-capacity contingency**, and **(c) appraisal contingency** (so a low VA Notice of
  Value doesn't force gap cash).
- Pursue **seller concessions up to 4%** (45 DOM + price cut = leverage) toward funding fee,
  prepaids, or a **rate buydown**.
- **Consider 5% down** if cash allows: it drops the **VA funding fee 2.15%→1.50%** (~$2.9–3.2k

- **Confirm VA disability status** — any rating ≥10% **fully waives** the funding fee (~$6.6–9.9k).
- Only go to **$450k** if permits confirm the apartment is legal **and** the appraisal/CMA supports it.
