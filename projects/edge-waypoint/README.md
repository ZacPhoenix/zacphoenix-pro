# Edge Waypoint - Founder's Kit

A complete operating kit for launching Edge Waypoint: a local, relationship-driven consultancy that helps small-to-medium businesses find their highest-value problems and solve them with the *minimum viable architecture* - SaaS, low/no-code, process fixes, or AI, in that order of consideration, never the other way around.

## How to use this kit

1. Read 01 and 03 first - the business plan tells you what you're selling, the frameworks are what you're actually selling.
2. Copy `starter-kit/` to a new private repo (e.g. `edge-waypoint-ops`) - that becomes your Claude Code working environment for running the business.
3. Book your first three Waypoint Workshops using the outreach scripts in 02. Everything downstream flows from workshops on the calendar.

## The one-sentence versions

- **What Edge Waypoint is:** a local guide who finds the few operational problems worth solving and solves them with the smallest thing that works.
- **What Edge Waypoint is not:** an AI agency. AI is rung 5 of a 6-rung solution ladder, and most SMB problems are solved at rungs 1-4.
- **Why clients buy:** "He told us NOT to buy AI for two of our three problems. That's why we trust him with the third."
