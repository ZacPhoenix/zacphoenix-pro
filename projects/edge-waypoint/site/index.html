What working with me looks like

  1. Waypoint 01 · 20 minutes · free

    First we talk.

    Twenty minutes on the phone. You tell me what's bugging you. If I don't think I can help, I'll say so on that call and probably point you at something cheaper than me.

  2. Waypoint 02 · 90 minutes · at your place

    Then I come see it.

    If it makes sense, I spend ninety minutes at your place with you and the people who actually do the work. We walk through where the hours go and put rough dollar figures on the worst of it. You keep the write-up whether you ever hire me or not.

  3. Waypoint 03 · 4 weeks · fixed price

    Then we fix one thing.

    Not ten things. One, in four weeks, for a fixed price we agree up front. We measure how the work went before, and how it goes after. If the new way isn't clearly better, I'll tell you to stop, in writing, and I won't charge you extra to find out.
