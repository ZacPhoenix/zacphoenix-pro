# Discovery Workshop — Acme Plumbing Supplies

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Present:** Consultant; Dawn (Owner); Raj (Sales admin); Mia (Warehouse lead); Tom (Finance)

---

**Consultant:** Walk me through what happens from when an order comes in.

**Raj:** Orders come three ways — email, the odd phone call, and our website form. I
re-type all of them into the ERP by hand. It's maybe 35 a day. Honestly about a third
have something wrong by the time I'm done — wrong SKU, wrong quantity. Takes me a few
minutes each but they pile up; an order might sit a few hours before I get to it.

**Consultant:** Then what?

**Mia:** I check if we've got stock. Usually quick, but it can wait a couple of hours if
we're busy on the floor.

**Dawn:** And if it's a quote, it comes to me to approve. Every single one, even the
tiny ones. Sometimes that sits the best part of a day waiting for me.

**Mia:** Once it's approved we pick and pack — that's the real work, half an hour or so,
and we're good at it, rarely a mistake.

**Tom:** Then I invoice. I re-key it from the order again into the accounting system.
Lots of corrections — call it 40% need a fix — and payment can lag a day or more.
Sometimes there's a billing dispute and it bounces all the way back to Raj.

**Consultant:** Let's talk restocking.

**Mia:** There's no real system. I notice we're low when I see a gap on the shelf, which
might be two days after we actually ran low. Then Purchasing raises a PO, which itself
takes half a day to go out.

**Dawn:** That gap is costing us — we've lost sales waiting on stock.

**Consultant:** Useful. I'll map this and we'll look at where the time really goes.
