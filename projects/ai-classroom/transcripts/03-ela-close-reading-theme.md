## Introduction

**Content.** A close-reading lesson using the folktale *The Empty Pot*. Students
move from plot recall to **theme** — the underlying message — and practice
grounding an interpretation in **specific text evidence**. Vocabulary in focus:
*courage*, *honesty*, *approval*.

**Agenda.**
1. Vocabulary preview with visuals + sentence frames (5 min)
2. Teacher read-aloud of the excerpt (8 min)
3. Think-pair-share: "What does the boy value more than winning?" (10 min)
4. Whole-group evidence discussion — cite the line (12 min)
5. Independent quick-write: state a theme + one quote (8 min)
6. Share-out and close (2 min)

**Structure.** Deliberately inclusive: visuals and sentence frames scaffold the
multilingual learner, think-pair-share gives every student a rehearsal before
public talk, and a written quick-write lets quiet students demonstrate depth.
This block is where **Student C** shines and where **Teacher 2's** inclusive
moves — and pacing edge — are visible.

**Learning outcomes.** Students will (1) state a theme of the text in a complete
sentence, and (2) support it with one accurately quoted detail. **Success
criterion:** the quick-write contains a theme statement and a correct quotation
with quotation marks.
