## Introduction

**Content.** The daily 15-minute advisory block that opens the 5th-grade day.
It is not academic instruction; it is community-building, logistics, and a brief
social-emotional check-in. It also establishes a baseline read on each student's
morning energy and mood before content instruction begins.

**Agenda.**
1. Attendance and settling in (2 min)
2. Morning announcements + Pledge of Allegiance (3 min)
3. "Rose & Thorn" SEL check-in — one good thing, one hard thing (7 min)
4. Preview of the day; distribute a permission slip (3 min)

**Structure.** Whole-group, teacher-facilitated, with an optional low-stakes
share. Sharing is invited but not required, which surfaces the difference
between *participation* and *engagement* right at the start of the day.

**Learning outcomes.** Students will (a) transition calmly into the school day,
(b) practice naming an emotion or experience, and (c) know the day's plan.
For the research system, this block establishes each student's **morning
baseline**: mood, readiness, and voluntary talk.
