## Introduction

**Content.** Students locate fractions on a number line, focusing on partitioning
a whole into equal parts and recognizing equivalent fractions (e.g., 1/2 = 2/4 =
3/6). This builds on prior work with fraction models (area/bar models) and moves
toward comparing fractions with unlike denominators.

**Agenda.**
1. Warm-up: label a number line 0 to 1 (5 min)
2. Review last night's homework — common sticking points (8 min)
3. Mini-lesson: partitioning and equivalence on the line (10 min)
4. Guided practice: place 3/4 and 2/3 together (8 min)
5. Partner practice: fraction card sort onto number lines (10 min)
6. Exit ticket: place 5/6 and explain (4 min)

**Structure.** Gradual-release ("I do / we do / you do") with a partner segment.
Mixes direct instruction (where lecture-format engagement patterns show) with
collaborative work (where format-dependent students re-engage).

**Learning outcomes.** Students will be able to (1) partition a number line into
equal parts, (2) place a given fraction accurately, and (3) identify at least one
equivalent fraction for a placed value. **Success criterion:** correctly place
5/6 on the exit ticket and justify the spacing.
