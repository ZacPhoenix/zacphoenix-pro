Try at home
🧊

Ride the science excitement

F loved the states-of-matter lab. Ask them to show you "condensation" on a cold glass of water — where does the water come from?

💪

Praise the effort, not "being smart"

When math feels hard, try "I saw you keep trying that" instead of "you're so smart." It helps F see mistakes as normal.

🗣️

Let them be the teacher

F explains ideas well out loud. Ask them to teach you one thing from social studies this week — talking it through builds confidence.

📖

Small math wins

Cooking and dividing snacks are low-pressure fractions practice. Keep it light — the goal is "math can feel okay," not drilling.
